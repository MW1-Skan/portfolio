### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer at [Padoa](https://www.padoa.fr/)**, building France's leading occupational-health platform — regulated health data (HDS, ISO 27001) covering millions of employees, shipped through event-driven microservices, Redis queues, and Kubernetes.

Before that: consulting at Viveris Systèmes, where I went from **intern to lead developer in under two years**, driving a 300k+ LOC monorepo and secure mobile apps to production.

*   🔭 Right now at work: shipping the compliance flow for the **French e-invoicing reform** — bulk imports for hundreds of thousands of companies, against a legal deadline.
*   🌱 In the lab: **AI engineering on Google Cloud** — ML services on Cloud Run, Vertex AI + Gemini, and agentic workflows with the Claude Agent SDK.
*   ⚡ **Core Philosophy**: "Time-to-Alpha" should not compromise architectural integrity.
